Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 3, 2025

Pets Care Pro ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Pet Information: Name, type (dog/cat), breed, birth date, gender, weight, and photos of your pets
  • Health Data: Symptoms you describe, AI health analysis results, and veterinary visit records
  • Calendar Events: Scheduled appointments, reminders, and care activities

Automatically Collected Information:

  • Usage Analytics: How you interact with the app (screens viewed, features used)
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, app version
  • Crash Reports: Technical information to help us fix bugs and improve stability

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide App Functionality: Store your pet profiles, track events, and deliver AI health analysis
  • Improve Our Services: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and user experience
  • Send Notifications: Remind you about scheduled events (with your permission)
  • Process Subscriptions: Manage your Premium subscription through the App Store

Data Storage and Security

Your pet data is stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We do not upload your pet profiles or photos to our servers.

When you use the AI Health Analysis feature, your pet's photo and symptom description are sent to OpenAI's API for processing. This data is not stored permanently and is used solely to generate health insights.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics: To understand app usage and improve features
  • Firebase Crashlytics: To detect and fix technical issues
  • OpenAI API: To provide AI-powered health analysis
  • Adapty: To manage subscription purchases
  • AppsFlyer: To measure app install attribution

Each service has its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review them.

Your Rights

  • Access: View all data stored in the app
  • Delete: Remove your pets, events, or entire app data at any time
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics collection in your device settings
  • Control Notifications: Manage notification preferences in app settings

Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
